# 7. aWavegent Booking — Zero-Fee Booking Engine

**"Real-time booking. Zero platform fees."**

For boat rentals, fishing charters, photographers, tours, and any activity business.

## The Problem It Attacks
> *"You're losing bookings to platforms that charge 6% of your revenue."*

- 🔒 **Platform owns your customers** — they control the relationship, reviews, and rebooking

## The 4-Step Booking Flow
1. **Scan QR or tap link** — from your GCR listing, your website, or the QR on your dock sign. Opens instantly in browser.
2. **Pick date & service** — live availability calendar, select the boat/charter/session/activity, pricing shown upfront
3. **Enter details & pay** — name, group size, contact info, secure Stripe payment. **No account required. No redirect.**
4. **Instant confirmation** — customer gets SMS + email, dashboard updates in real time, **zero platform fees taken**

## Features
- 📅 **Live availability calendar** — block times, set durations, manage multiple boats or services; customers only see real open slots
- 💳 **Stripe payments** — full payment or deposit at booking, refund policies built in, funds go direct to your Stripe account
- 📝 **Digital waivers** — signed at booking time, stored automatically, no paper, no chasing signatures at the dock
- 📍 **Multiple docks / locations** — run from multiple launch points, customers pick their dock, directions sent automatically

# 8. CyberMate — Patent-Pending Verified Reviews

**"Real purchase. Real review. Real trust."** · *Patent pending.*

The verified trust layer for every transaction. Positioned as a **universal cross-platform standard**, not a single-marketplace feature.

## Vision
> Restore trust in online reviews by tying every review to a real purchase. One-time token via SMS/email ensures only verified customers can review. Works across **Square, Clover, Toast, Stripe, Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon.**

## The Problem
- Fake reviews (bots, paid, competitor sabotage) dominate Yelp, Google, Amazon
- No cross-platform way to prove a review came from a real purchase
- **85% of consumers don't trust online reviews** because they know they can be faked
- Competitor 1-star attacks with no receipt required
- Low review volume — customers forget, the link is buried, friction kills follow-through
- Google and Yelp have no idea if the reviewer ever bought anything

## POS Flow
1. Customer pays at POS (Square, Clover, Toast, Stripe)
2. Transaction ID + phone captured
3. CyberMate sends one-time SMS link
4. Customer leaves verified review
5. Review published with **Verified badge**

## E-Commerce Flow
1. Customer orders online (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon)
2. System **waits until product is delivered**
3. CyberMate sends secure SMS/email
4. Only that customer can review
5. Review tagged *"Verified by Purchase – Shopify"* (etc.)

## Collection Methods
POS-triggered invites · E-commerce delayed invites · QR/NFC codes on receipts or tables · Booking/service follow-ups · CRM automations · Direct review links

## Full 8-Step End-to-End Flow
**POS Order → Receipt Data → Secure Invite → Item Review → Server Review → Verified Badge → Business Insights → AI Suggestions**

## Item-Level & Server-Level Reviews
- Every review tied to **individual items and servers**
- Customers rate specific dishes, boats, staff, or services — not just overall experience
- Server ratings create data for service-level insights
- Item-level analytics: best dishes, service quality

## AI Recommendations Layer
> AI analyzes preferences to recommend similar dishes at **other** restaurants.
> **Example:** Loved Grilled Red Snapper at Cobalt's → Try Grouper Almondine at GT's.

Predictive recommendations increase loyalty and repeat visits.

## POS Integration & Digital Receipts
- Payments integrated directly with POS systems like Toast
- Seamless loyalty program sign-up tied to transactions
- Automatic digital receipts with items, prices, date, location
- **Receipts stored digitally — useful for taxes and compliance**
- Links reviews, payments, and loyalty into one unified platform

## Competitive Edge
| Competitor | Their weakness | CyberMate |

| **Yelp** | Fake reviews, ad bias | Transaction-verified |
| **Google** | Easily manipulated | Verified by POS, every industry |

# 13. Daily Menu Update System

**"Your menu. Updated in 60 seconds."**

## The Problem
> *"Every restaurant's menu online is wrong. And every platform told them to 'just log in and update it.' Nobody does. So the data stays stale."*

- ❌ **Wrong prices everywhere** — "Menu online says $18. Actual price is $24. Customer feels misled before they even sit down."
- 📵 **No one logs into dashboards** — "Owners are cooking at 6am"
- 🐟 **Catch of the day? Yesterday's** — seafood spots change every morning, menus show last week
- 📉 **Specials no one sees** — Tuesday taco special runs 3 months, shows online for the first week
- 💬 **"What's the market price?"** — customers shouldn't have to ask

## Three Steps. No App. No Login.
1. **You send them a link** — generate a secure link from admin dashboard, text it to the owner. Takes 10 seconds.
2. **They tap it on their phone** — full menu opens, pre-loaded with current data. No login, no password.
3. **Changes go live instantly** — price update, new special, today's catch — saved immediately to QR menu and GCR profile

## The Link Mechanics
- Link is **unique to their phone number** — no one else can use it
- **Expires after 30 hours** so it's always fresh
- **One link per business per day** — automatically managed
- Fully automated once SMS provider is approved
