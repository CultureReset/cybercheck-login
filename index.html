The real difference

Most platforms want to become the middleman.

You shouldn't have to abandon the software that already runs your business just to get better customer capture, bookings, reviews, and follow-up.

The marketplace model

They bring the customer — then keep the leverage.

  • The marketplace controls the customer experience
  • You get pushed toward a new checkout and workflow
  • Customer data and attribution stay trapped
  • You pay commissions building someone else's platform
  • Reviews, loyalty, and follow-up stay fragmented
The CyberCheck model

We add the layer around what you already run.

  • Keep the booking system, POS, CRM, or website you use
  • Send customers to your existing checkout
  • Capture who they are before and after the handoff
  • One customer record for service, reviews, and loyalty
  • Everything modular — turn on only what you need
